SERVICE AGREEMENT
This Service Agreement is made on 1 April 2026 between Zenith Retail Pvt Ltd ("Client") and CloudNova Solutions ("Vendor").
1. Services. Vendor shall provide cloud hosting and maintenance services for the Client's e-commerce platform.
2. Fees. Client shall pay INR 18,00,000 per year, invoiced quarterly, payable within 45 days of invoice.
3. Term. This Agreement starts on 1 April 2026 and expires on 31 March 2027. It renews automatically for one year unless either party gives notice 90 days before expiry, i.e. by 31 December 2026.
4. Termination. Vendor may terminate at any time with 15 days notice. Client may terminate only for material breach after a 60 day cure period.
5. Confidentiality. Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret for 2 years.
6. Intellectual Property. All custom work product is owned by the Vendor.
7. Liability. Vendor's liability is unlimited. Client's liability is limited to fees paid in the last month.
8. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Delaware, USA.
